# Sources and media

- `go2_walk.py` is copied unchanged from `ros/go2_walk.py` in `sriram-2502/go2-webrtc-gesture-nav` at commit `a8c63d630f3abf0a0528cf4c7fe2c453af6b87b6`.
- The setup/recording guide is adapted from the same source. This repository extracts the ROS path without the WebRTC or Clemson application.
- `docs/assets/go2-controller.jpg` is the controller photograph previously supplied for the lab setup.
- The Ethernet photograph and velocity clip were supplied by the lab maintainer on 2026-11-04; the maintainer confirmed the photographed robot is Go2 EDU.
- `docs/assets/go2-jetson-ethernet.png` is the maintainer's annotated Ethernet photograph, resized to 1200 px wide with source metadata removed. The annotated original remains local.
- `docs/assets/go2-velocity-demo.gif` is an FFmpeg-generated 8 fps / 320 px wide / 64-color GIF of the complete roughly 3.1-second clip, without audio or source metadata. It loops; each loop is not a new robot trial. The exact command and firmware were not recorded in the clip metadata.
- Full-resolution originals remain local. Conversion details and hashes are recorded in `docs/assets/README.md`.
